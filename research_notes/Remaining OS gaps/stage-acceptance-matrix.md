## Key Question 1: What does docs/master-development-roadmap.md define as the required stages and acceptance gates?

### Takeaway
The roadmap defines a sequential Stage 10.7 → 36 program across 24 phases; acceptance is deliberately "bounded software/QEMU" only, with native hardware, power-loss, production-trust and independent-review gates kept separate. AGENTS.md adds mandatory feature-gating, Clippy and single-chain execution rules; the Stage 1–19 closure ledger is explicitly BLOCKED / NOT PRODUCTION-READY.

### Inferences
- "Accepted" in this repository is nearly always scoped: a bounded software/QEMU contract at a named source baseline, not full stage or release acceptance. The roadmap, AGENTS.md and closure ledger all forbid promoting QEMU evidence into hardware/trust claims.
- The stage numbering exceeds Stage 36 in practice (24.x, 10.x, 13.x, 19.x, 20.x, 27.x, 28, 29, 31, 32 sub-stages); stage slices are accepted individually even while the collective Stage 1–19 closure and Stages 33–36 remain open.

### Gaps
- No record in `stage-status.md` was found claiming acceptance of Stages 33 (Alpha), 34 (Beta), 35 (RC) or 36 (1.0); the roadmap defines them aspirationally.
- The roadmap does not contain a single compact per-stage pass/fail table; the status must be reconstructed from `stage-status.md` and release-qualification records.
